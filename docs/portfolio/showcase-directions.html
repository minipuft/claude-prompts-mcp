A / Route through an evolving landscape

RESTRAINED BLUEPRINT · LEADING STUDY
Spatial idea

A quiet topography acquires a route and a checkpoint. The world is a visible record of authored practice.

Motion

A context packet travels; an issue holds it; an explicit authoring annotation adds structure. A second packet reuses the prepared path.

Risk / reversal

Landscape can imply self-growing intelligence. Reject it if the authoring action becomes invisible or viewers infer automatic learning.

B / A studio built through authorship

EDITORIAL WORKSHOP · SERIOUS COUNTER-VECTOR
Spatial idea

Prompt rooms and review desks give the work an address. Authorship assembles a small studio, rather than an autonomous city.

Motion

The author places a desk where the previous task exposed a gap. Later work visits that desk on its way to the next room.

Risk / reversal

Buildings can become a distracting taxonomy. Reduce to a workshop if viewers ask what a district means before they understand the check.

C / The task as a transit journey

LITERAL TRACK · BOUNDARY STUDY
Spatial idea

The request is the vehicle; prompt routes and authored checkpoints make the journey readable. The rollercoaster suggestion becomes a restrained track study.

Motion

The camera follows the packet, not a thrill ride. Show track construction separately from traversal; later work encounters the already-authored checkpoint.

Risk / reversal

A literal ride can imply inevitability or guaranteed output. Keep this as the counterexample if transit spectacle dominates deliberate authorship.

Observation → interpretation → transfer

The current teal line and amber dot already suggest route and attention. Transfer those properties into explicit context movement and authored checks. Do not recolor or distort the mascot, or let decorative signals masquerade as observed execution.

Evidence boundary

The SVG worlds are speculative illustrations. Agent work does not automatically write prompts, gates, or frameworks. A client or author explicitly creates and versions the reusable resources. Frameworks guide reasoning; they are not literal physics or universal enforcement.
