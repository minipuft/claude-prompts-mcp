## Shared drawing and color starting point

Proposed drawing law: **each mark explains silhouette, overlap, material or intent; light organizes forms into designed value planes while texture stays subordinate.**

Weight selected silhouette, contact/occlusion and compressed-fold edges; taper or omit selected lit/open edges. Every shaded edge need not thicken. Cloth folds follow suspension, tension and compression; hair uses unequal coherent masses. Separate pigment/substrate from illumination and cast/contact shadows.

Neutral construction-study baseline: soft upper-left open daylight, warm filtered key and cool sky fill, quiet bone/ivory background and low-chroma human cloth. Final palette remains open. The owner requests inspecting `/home/minipuft/Applications/cloudySky` color/lighting pipeline and quality baseline as a potential transfer. For later integration, test sage world and muted plum/slate garment masses against Codex blue and Clawd coral. An overcast bone/grey-green ink field remains an earlier comparison candidate; new palette candidates must consider the CloudySky source audit. Exact pigments and final color keys remain open; human coral is not locked. Source/grade, scene lighting, readable value/identity relationships and display output must be explicit. CloudySky's dark additive glass/emitter grammar and UI numeric floors are not automatically animation requirements.

Final color script follows shot purpose, light direction/source, value hierarchy and cast separation beat by beat. Early color relationships can parallel construction; final keys depend on the selected human, integrated trio/world composition and storyboard. No grade/filter can repair anatomy or contact.
