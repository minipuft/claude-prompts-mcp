# Beat 4 seuss 05 — Midnight Paintings architecture behind the glass (D50)

Rendered 2026-10-07 after six Seuss references were filed (`references/seuss-*`). [full](beat4-seuss-05-f090.png) 7c40a1695ff847aa…, [frame 3x](beat4-seuss-05-frame.png), copied unchanged from `out/`.

Built `src/library/glass/seuss.tsx`: flat silhouettes in depth with a pale contour one step behind (`Silhouette`), hand-authored blobby tower profiles (`blobShape`), receding turned frame rings (`NestedFrames`, from the nested-frames painting), curling stalks with flag leaves (`Stalk`), and a beaded light string (`LightString`). The field is now black with one grained blue cloud mass and sparse colored stars. The kit boxes are gone from the interior.

Holds: the interior reads as a tunnel of receding frames, which is the first time the "world within" has depth that is drawn rather than lit; towers carry pale contours on dark, the Seuss outline language; the glass adds chromatic fringes to the rings that read as someplace else rather than as a defect; grain is now subordinate.

Fails: the three towers are too large and too close, blocking the tunnel and the sky; the ground is a flat grey slab with no stipple; the stalks' leaves are too small to read and the light string is hidden behind the towers; the emitter is invisible; the kit palette is still the pale daylight sample, so the towers read sage and cream where the paintings are blue, cream and red on black; the frame rings' fringing may be stronger than wanted.
