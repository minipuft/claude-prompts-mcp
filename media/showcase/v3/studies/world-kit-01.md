## Visible shape and medium review

Observed after generation and after reopening the saved PNG:
- Top tray depicts six families: soft ground patch, curved path, open pavilion, worktable, display panel, folding shutter.
- A clusters table/panel/shutter inside or beside the pavilion. B moves the table to a left pocket and panel/shutter to a right pocket, joined by branching routes. C opens a larger gathering court with the table central and pavilion/panel/shutter redistributed.
- Unequal pavilion canopy and curved supports, rounded footprint boundaries and soft surface masses communicate an authored asymmetric family. The structures repeat recognizably across the three scenes.
- Trio appears in all layouts. Human retains swept dark hair, taupe sleeveless outer shirt/pale inner sleeves and dark loose trousers; blue screen-faced Codex and coral block Clawd remain identifiable.
- Daylight direction/material colors and three-quarter viewing direction are broadly consistent. No dense forest, rigid square/hex grid, extra cast, terminal, frontend or server-concept text appears; only A/B/C are labelled.

Visible deviations / unresolved invariants:
- Stools, loose papers/blocks, foliage and rocks are added beyond the six commissioned families; expansion is not limited to declared patch/path duplicates. Same-kit inventory is therefore NOT demonstrated exactly.
- Low ground pieces have clearly outlined raised rims and scenic edge plants. Routes connect the places visually, but the presentation still tends toward separate assembled dioramas; subtle continuously joined ground is only partially supported.
- Companion placement changes from table height to ground and back. Pose/projection changes prevent a verified constant actor-height/camera-scale claim.
- Pavilion, table and panel contours vary slightly between layouts. Recognizable family resemblance is not immutable asset reuse or connector fit proof.
- Shading is softly painted with some grouped planes; contour weight is fairly restrained. This does not yet prove the requested cel/occlusion/line hierarchy under changing light or movement.
