## What each world is

| World | Figure | Props | Field and light |
|---|---|---|---|
| home | `MeshFigure` untouched (a) | flat roles, near-black outline, stipple | `#07060c`, warm radiance masked to the join |
| crayon | the flat render through one SVG filter (grow the alpha by the contour width in the dark ink, displace by low-frequency noise, knock specks out with grain; the noise seed follows the boil, 6/s) | the same filter, strokes at the contract's width | paper `#f3ead8` with its own grain, warm radiance |
| line | the mesh's boundary edges as screen-space fat lines (one closed loop by construction); no fill | strokes only: fills cleared, stipple hidden, inner shapes outlined | `#07060c`, radiance off |
| shaded | one fragment program: N-step light from the texture's own alpha height field, cool shadow step, emissive silhouette stroke; perspective camera, the figure turned 24° | architecture and blocks extruded with `MeshToonMaterial` (2-step gradient map) and emissive fat-line edges; companions are the home sprites | `#05070d`, cold radiance |

`reduce` (0 world → 1 contour only) is the one control the crossing adds to every Scene. Fills fade by opacity, strokes are redrawn at `figureHeight/ratio` (4.03 px) in the line colour and stay, so no render is dissolved against a copy of itself.

## Crossing

`<Crossing from="home" to="shaded" start={30} length={70} lead={[56, 0]} />` in thirds: frames 30–53 home reduces (fills out, lines stay, field eases to the line field), 53–77 the line world holds alone and slides 56 px right along the leading line, 77–100 the lines slide back as shaded refills (the figure turns to three-quarter while its fill returns). Everything steps on twos; fills under 8 % or over 92 % snap to 0 or 1 (a 3–15 % stipple fill left 18–40 px specks that read as extra silhouette pieces on frames 50–51). Frames 78–81 failed for another reason, found in the same run: the shaded world's three.js canvas was exactly 720 px tall and clipped the raised hands, which now have 240 px of canvas margin above and below.

## Seen on the frames

- **Crayon reads.** `world-sheet-crayon.png`: dark wobbling contour, grain in every fill, paper under it. The grain is dense at tooth 0.35; at 1:1 it reads as pastel on cartridge paper rather than wax. Cream hair meets cream paper and is carried by the contour alone.
- **Line reads as a contour drawing of the props and a silhouette of the human.** The default draws no face: the eyes and the poncho's colour wedges are inner contours (`line.interior`), which turn the drawing into islands (c3). With them on, `world-sheet-line.png` earlier in the work showed the face and the wedges, with blotchy lines where the texture's stipple crosses a colour edge.
- **Shaded is a card, not a volume.** The figure is the flat drawing turned 24° in perspective with two lighting steps along its rim; the steps read as a cel-shaded rim, not as form. The emissive edge is a flat `#457ca4` stroke with no bloom: it reads as a blue outline, not a glow. The architecture (extruded frame, two blocks) does show depth under the perspective.
- The mesh outline traces the silhouette polygon where the mask has a narrow cut (hair against collar, tie), so loops appear inside the figure in line and shaded.

### What changed per world

- **crayon.** Tooth is 0.18 and the grain is gone. Two noise images made in a frame rotated to `stroke.angle` (−75°, low frequency along the stroke, high across it) work on the fills inside the same SVG filter: a hatch knocks ink out in sparse streaks along one angle and a tone lightens and darkens the wax along them. The contour is a solid ring, `contourPx` wide, outside the silhouette, so where the hatch takes a fill out paper shows, not ink. The human sits inside `STAGE.figureCrop` and takes `figureStrength` 0.7 of both images; companions, frame and blocks take the same 0.7 of the hatch and none of the tone, so Codex's and Clawd's fills keep their pigment. The arch, gates and floor are scribbled strokes (`crayonStrokes.ts`) at the world's full hatch. The noise images are rasterised once per boil variant before the first frame is captured, which is what brought the cost from about 1 s to 0.7 s.
- **shaded.** An emissive fat-line grid on the plane y = 480 under the perspective camera; three flat annulus gates, brightness 0.38, 0.62, 1 with their glow scaled by its square, each mirrored below the floor at low opacity; the arch as an extruded solid, its front face on the plane z = 0 so it lands on `STAGE.arch`'s pixels and the stone recedes behind it. Bloom is two more fat lines behind every emissive edge, at 3 × and 7 × the width and 0.28 and 0.10 opacity, additive, no new dependency (`shading.bloom` in the contract). The figure's light is read from a wider height field (46 texels, mip 5.5, gain 3) so its far side falls to the shadow step across the poncho, the face and the hands, not only along a thin rim; `lightDir`, `shadowFloor` 0.52 and `stepAt` 0.5 are contract keys.
- **line.** `line.interior` is true: the face reads. The arch, gates and floor are strokes at the contour weight. `measure-worlds.py loop` replaces the component count: it hole-fills the ink and calls the loop closed when the largest filled body spans the full height of everything drawn and holds at least 95 % of the filled area, so eyes and hair, which a closed outline encloses, are absorbed.
- **WorldSheet.** Worlds with an arch print their contract in a translucent strip along the bottom; home keeps its panel at the top left (it has no arch, and its figure crop is the byte-identity probe). The crayon panel's text was pale on paper (the dark-text test compared the field to a hex the crayon world does not use); it is now dark.

### Open concerns

1. **Arch and the leading line.** The crossing slides the whole drawing up to 56 px right, so the arch's right edge (370 at rest) enters the figure crop from about frame 56 to about frame 96. `loop` leaves out filled pieces that touch the crop's left border; the figure's cape tip stays 40 px inside it. In the shaded phase the arch's glow tail reaches about x 384, four pixels into the crop.
2. **The stage pops.** The line world draws the arch, gates and floor as strokes only during the hold, so they appear at its start and the shaded refill replaces them with geometry; the crayon world is not in the proof crossing.
3. **Crayon pigments** are at 63 % and 61 % of the flat render's exact-colour counts. Lower `figureStrength` or the hatch level to trade grain for identity; the plate's companions are cleaner than the plate's world.
4. **Crayon's fast path** needs the rasterised noise: a strength other than rest (a crossing out of or into crayon) builds new images and falls back to live SVG images, about 0.3 s a frame slower. The current crossing does not use it.
5. **Shaded halo.** The figure's silhouette glows in a 14 px halo at the contract's bloom numbers; the plate's figure has none. The numbers were fixed by the brief and are the next dial.
6. **Timing is noisy.** The box runs other sessions; the same home code read 0.12–0.19 s here and 0.11–0.14 s earlier. Quote the three runs, not one.
