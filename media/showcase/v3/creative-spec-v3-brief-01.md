## Settled intent

Audience: developers already using AI coding agents. A silent spectator episode follows a stylized human, original blue terminal-faced Codex companion and coral Claude Code Clawd together from the opening. They are comfortable friends in an inhabited, playful alien world, with more to discover. Later specialists can join.

The demonstration concerns making this video and scaffolding its reusable creative workflow through Claude Prompts MCP. Real client/server interactions need actual captured evidence. Symbolic scenes are illustrative and cannot establish live provider activity, automatic learning or actual verification.

The world should feel soft and organic with designed cel values and intentional authored contours. The hand-authored feeling belongs to the drawing and surfaces, not literal drawing powers. Reuse the owner's Adventure Time / Ni no Kuni / Okami reference roles and exact inspected demo properties; preserve source pointers in the research receipts.

## Human comparison invariants

Preserve accepted compact figure intent, wide-set eyes, large forehead, small nose and tiny full lips. Face/body intent comes from the right-hand human in [compact sheet02](../v2/assets/human-compact-02.png); [hair/drape sheet01](../v2/assets/hair-drape-01.png) is a starting hair-attitude reference and failed unchanged garment control. These are references, not approved final pixels or mandatory pivots.

Contrast a rebellious careless hair silhouette with ordinary yet sophisticated clothing. Change garment cut, suspension, overlap and hair masses. Retain hanging drape while making shoulder/armhole, forearm/cuff and hand contact readable. Compare2–3 directions under the same face, view, pose, light and neutral cloth value roles. A sheet can support construction review; it cannot establish temporal motion.

Current proposed construction lead: offset-panel pullover, defined shoulder/armhole, controllable forearm, layered diagonal hem and uneven swept crest. Longer raglan shirt and short sleeveless outer layer remain comparison alternatives. No construction is locked before the actual sheet is inspected.

## Shared drawing and color starting point

Proposed drawing law: **each mark explains silhouette, overlap, material or intent; light organizes forms into designed value planes while texture stays subordinate.**

Weight selected silhouette, contact/occlusion and compressed-fold edges; taper or omit selected lit/open edges. Every shaded edge need not thicken. Cloth folds follow suspension, tension and compression; hair uses unequal coherent masses. Separate pigment/substrate from illumination and cast/contact shadows.

Comparison baseline: soft upper-left open daylight, warm filtered key and cool sky fill, quiet bone/ivory background and low-chroma human cloth. For later integration, test sage world and muted plum/slate garment masses against Codex blue and Clawd coral. An overcast bone/grey-green ink field is the alternative if daylight loses form or identity. Exact pigments and final color keys remain open; human coral is not locked.

Final color script follows shot purpose, light direction/source, value hierarchy and cast separation beat by beat. Early color relationships can parallel construction; final keys depend on the selected human, integrated trio/world composition and storyboard. No grade/filter can repair anatomy or contact.

## First motion-proof proposal

One short readable action: turn → reach/lift a replaceable object → contact → companion response/correction → settle, with cloth/hair response and one small world-scaffolding beat. Duration6–10s is a workload proposal, not a quality threshold. Exact object, symbolic gate vocabulary and full-film story beats remain unsettled.

Require independent acting, clear contact and consecutive-frame inspection. Repeat after one declared object/garment-slot replacement while holding motion inputs constant where the method permits it. Re-registration/redrawing is explicit when required. Technique selection follows `tech_recommendation` against this proof, with appropriate tools at each stage.
