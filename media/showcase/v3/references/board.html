Raw capability is radiant and overwhelming; scaffolding is one lit join at a time. Saturated figures on a black field, Seuss architecture, Midnight Gospel shadows, drawn on twos with light on ones.

Timing at 30 fps

hold8–20nothing moves but boil and light
anticipation3–5small move opposite the action

settle10–16damped return, wobble decaying

Laws

  1. One actor moves at a time; related elements stagger 3–5 frames.
  2. No linear curve anywhere: poses step, light springs with memory.
  3. Boil is not shimmer: seeded variants at ~4/s, never a per-frame reseed.
  4. Light lags drawing by 2–4 frames; glass lags light by 2–4 more.
  5. Every move exposes cause and recovery; speed is never a move's identity.
  6. No text moves. Identity pigments never grade.
  7. The fewest cuts: beats progress by morphs and camera moves along leading lines the world provides (D64).

Medium

  • Remotion is the single substrate; the frame interior is a Three.js glass pass mounted through @remotion/three (D41).
  • This board holds references, laws and lineage only; nothing the project drew (D56).
  • No captions beyond the held title, planner assumption under D53 (D57).
  • Frame bars gold from the nested-frames painting, planner assumption (D58).

Process

  1. No asset enters a scene as its first iteration: a muse sheet of 3+ variants, each citing a card here, comes first (D55).
  2. Two chains: showcase_preproduction once per episode, showcase_shot once per shot; the shot list is the handoff (D59).
  3. A shot run feeds the script: measured ranges go back into shots.json and the beat (D60).
  4. A render study is a frame set with probes, never one still (D61).
  5. Blender is an explicit, confirmed, optional path on the render study (D62).
  6. Companions keep their pigments and morph their construction to the world (D63).
  7. Owner-library cards carry eagle://item/<ID> provenance and are filed through /inspiration; a reference cited anywhere in v3 without an id or URL is rejected by check-provenance (D74 fold, 2026-10-13).

What the film is not

  • The early-web explainer video: everything tweening at once on a linear curve, hinge-jointed cutouts, pop-in text, no holds, no anticipation, motion with no cause.
  • Calm daylight scenery with fill everywhere and no negative space.
  • A frame interior that is a flat gradient, a picture instead of a place.
  • A toon shader standing in for drawn shadow shapes.

2 · Shadow, grain and the gadget

Shadows are designed shapes: a form shadow following the silhouette and a cast shadow on the field, each with a weighted edge. The gadget is familiar parts in an impossible whole, like The Midnight Gospel's universe simulator.

4 · Animation language: drawn on twos, light on ones

The drawn layer (flat assets, contours, stipple) moves on twos with hold, anticipation, action, overshoot, settle, and boils visibly between seeded variants. The light layer (radiance, motes, glass, emitter) runs continuous on ones, lagging the drawing by 2–4 frames and recovering without snapping. Beats progress by cut and reframe, not pans.
