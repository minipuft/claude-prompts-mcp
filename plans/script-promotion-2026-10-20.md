Goal: every script that answers a fleet-wide question lives in repository-standards as a managed file; this repo keeps only what reads `src/`, `resources/` or its own CI shape; the plan-row stamp rules have one implementation that CI and the global `~/.claude` plan hooks both call.
Current slice: T3.2a (stamp pre-work, branch `chore/standards-plan-rows`, worktree `../claude-prompts-mcp-plan-rows`); 3.2 waits on repository-standards#51 (release 1.14.0, hard stop); 3.3 after 3.2.

Upstream managed scripts carry no runtime dependency (`package.json` has none; `js-yaml` must go). No `.sh`→JS conversions in this plan (assessment verdict: superseded or not worth it; `generate-brand-assets.py` stays Python).

## Why this exists

Assessment of 2026-10-20 (chat): no fleet member carries a twin of any candidate yet, so the upstream case rests on shared need. The delivery contract installs `.husky/commit-msg` in every member, so every member has the dead-hooks-in-a-worktree failure; every member has workflows (action pins), branch protection (required contexts) and release-please (changelog merge). `validate-plan-row-tracking.js` and `~/.claude/hooks/lib/plan_lint.py` were assessed as two derivations of one status vocabulary. ⚠ Measured 2026-10-20 (row 3.0): the shared part is the table parser and the glyph set, not the stamp rules — Python has no stamp rules to replace, and the parser exists in four places (the two above, `plan_hygiene.py`, and upstream's managed `validate-pr-body.mjs`). The JS one still runs only in this repo's CI.
