## A. Why (the sighting that cut this plan)

2026-09-27, cloudySky: a fifth-grade metaphor ("almost the foam of our water") became a mechanism without a stop; the sixth grade rejected it as character ("a layer underneath", "gel"). The muse then ended with three forks, the planner folded them into a build, and the first strip failed on exactly those forks. The owner asked for the boundary the same day: _"establish the interview as a better boundary … triggered or called more freely based on your interpretation of the current situation … stopping progress for an interview, utilizing our prompts/workflows to get better research or attempt a different path."_ Prior sightings in `~/.claude/observations.jsonl` (7 `interview` lines): an interview on an exploring operator is a hard stop per question (2026-09-06); the frontier round settled ten rulings in three rounds (2026-09-13).
