### F3 ruling (main thread, 2026-08-11, at Tier 5 open)

`{{step1_result}}` off-by-one: **document reality this phase; queue the semantic fix as its own backlog item.** P3's stance is byte-parity on everything rendered — changing emitted variable names (`step2_result` → `step1_result`) would break every existing template that adapted to the actual behavior, and grading that blast radius is its own discovery pass (template sweep across resources + downstream repos). Tier 5 docs state the real mapping (first step's output lands in `step2_result` under current arithmetic); the rename goes to the prompt-evolution/debt backlog with a template-sweep precondition.
