## Intent and completion criteria

**Work type:** feature, with a bounded contract refactor. **Confidence:** high on current owners and seams; medium on classification and calibration policy. **Risk:** high: false acceptance, evidence loss, private-case exposure, and confusing a model verdict with tool verification.

Current state: semantic guidance can be arbitrary prose. Existing self/judge routing specifies who reviews, and structured per-gate submissions carry an index, boolean and rationale before being flattened to text. Desired state: an explicitly authored semantic evaluation defines its target, questions, evidence, result domains and acceptance rules. An independently versioned suite tests those judgments; immutable validation records link the exact gate, suite, artifacts and reviewer.

One initiative, three linked objects: **gate definition -> calibration suite -> validation record**. Test cases are part of the gate's evolution from the first slice; suite updates and gate-definition updates remain distinct revisions.

**Contract invariants:**

- Refuse duplicate, unknown or missing criterion IDs; out-of-domain values; inconsistent state/value; mismatched gate, node, output, attempt or revision; and evidence spans/digests that do not resolve against the selected target.
- Keep insufficient evidence distinct from measured failure. Neither permits a blocking gate to advance. Existing advisory behavior may advance with an explicit warning and the actual observation retained.
- Preserve existing gate_action semantics, but record skip/alternative/override as bypass dispositions; they do not become successful calibration grades.
- A legacy PASS or reminder attestation cannot satisfy a gate that explicitly requires a structured semantic report. Give an actionable refusal naming the missing report; preserve legacy submissions for existing ordinary gates.
- A syntactically valid quote is not external factual verification. Content hashes bind bytes, not truth or provenance; report the review's actual evidence boundary.
- Freeze the resolved criterion contract at review opening; bind it to the actual captured target before adjudication. Edits during a pending review must not change what its result means.
- Attachments may expose public rubric anchors only. Reserved cases and expected answers remain outside MCP lists/inspect, prompt rendering, judge envelopes, resource snapshots, package assets and candidate-visible roots.

## Pre-flight and risk diagnosis

Identification: pure criterion/observation validation derives a decision; existing services own review lifecycle and I/O; an optional adapter conducts calibration using the existing durable archive. This does not require another coordinator.

Domain, reuse and layer placement were checked by source/ownership searches. No external library or provider SDK is selected. Compilation, runtime accuracy and new-code complexity are unmeasured until implementation.

Two concrete hazards: rich structured evidence is lost at today's normalization boundary, and the orchestrators involved are already large. GateVerdictProcessor has 1,230 lines, MCP tools/index.ts 1,460, StepResponseCaptureStage 1,360, ResponseAssembler 1,689, Chains manager 3,329, skills-sync/service.ts 5,205. Those counts identify decomposition pressure; they do not establish a new function's complexity.

**Diagnosis:** rich evidence crosses an oversized orchestration boundary. Put validation/acceptance in a small pure gate-owner kernel and extend narrow existing projections. Do not add semantic business logic to those large classes or perform unrelated wholesale decomposition. A worker must inspect the specific edited functions' lint/complexity baseline, decompose where needed, and stop if the row cannot satisfy the project thresholds within its bound.
