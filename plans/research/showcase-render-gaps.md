## Three candidates for a later `tech_recommendation`

| Candidate                                        | Concrete treatment to investigate                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Benefit / decisive risk / required first proof                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Authored NPR 3D with drawn strokes**           | Designed character/cloth/hair volumes; discrete light/shadow value planes; contour and selected crease/occlusion strokes, with authored taper, omissions and shadow-side emphasis. Painted pigment/substrate stays attached to surfaces. | Shared form/light/contact and replaceable items; modeling/rig/stroke-authoring cost, risk of generic toy geometry. Show the selected human's rest→reach→grasp with hair/cloth volume and a cel-world joint, not a mannequin/material sphere. Tool/API selection remains open.            |
| **2D animation and compositing**                 | Re-drawn key/extreme/contact poses, separate light/shadow shapes, authored line-weight/fold maps and controlled in-betweens; painted environment with designed value groups. Reuse spatial blockout as reference if helpful.             | Direct silhouette, cloth and stroke control; more pose labor and asset changes can require redraws. | **Hybrid video assistance with authored repair** | Approved pose/color boards and a reproducible scene/contact reference guide an assisted sequence; retain explicit mask/layer/repair and source provenance.                                                                               | Can explore richer motion quickly; unproven identity, palette, line and item-anchor consistency. Must prove consecutive-frame construction and a replaceable-item repeat before adoption. A generated attractive clip alone does not prove an editable rig or reproducible choreography. |

## Gate proposal: observable drawing before production expansion

These are **proposed criteria**, not installed gate IDs or artistic scores. Visual gates require opened proof and a recorded owner/planner judgment. Missing proof means review remains unresolved. Rejected controls must remain visibly identified. Executable checks can verify artifacts/invariants; they cannot independently judge drawing quality.

| Gate ID / judge                               | Observable criterion                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Required proof                                                                                                      | Negative control                                                                                      | Failure action                                                                           |
| --------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `showcase-cel-form` / visual                  | Character and world use designed discrete value planes; form, contact and focal hierarchy survive the same key/fill. Avoid accidental photographic gradients or flattened unreadable bands.                                                        | Same composition: approved value/color key, actual rendered rest and reach, lighting/form breakdown.                | Current continuous Lambert world + fixed unlit character; later candidate with wrong value grouping.  | Re-author planes/lighting or candidate; hold full scene.                                 |
| `showcase-line-construction` / visual         | Distinguish silhouette, occlusion, fold and selected form edges. Shadow-side weight supports mass/contact; taper, pressure and broken/lost edges serve composition. **Not every dark edge gets thicker**, and not every form edge needs outlining. | Annotated line-intent key and actual character/world crops, including lit and shadow sides plus contact.            | Uniform post-outline or constant tube substituted for the authored treatment; no-line comparison.     | Correct stroke selection/weight/construction before texture polish.                      |
| `showcase-gesture-cloth-hair` / visual        | Selected identity reads through deliberate gesture; ordinary drape forms believable volume/tension at shoulder, elbow and hem; careless hair masses move/settle coherently. Hand actually appears to grasp/reposition.                             | Actual rest, reach, stop-short, correction and release poses plus consecutive-frame playback.                       | Current chest sleeve overlap, fixed palm and plane-shear hem; candidate omitting cloth/hair response. | Rework assets/poses or rig, then re-register; no texture-filter fix.                     |

**Sequence:** approve pose/shape and color/line keys → canonical technique survey → smallest actual cross-medium contact/cloth/line-motion proof → owner/planner grade against these criteria → expand shot.
