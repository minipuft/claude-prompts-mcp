## Goals

**Proposed drawing law:** _Each mark explains silhouette, overlap, material or intent; light organizes those forms into designed value planes, while texture stays subordinate._

Give weight to sleeve-over-arm, cuff cavity, hair-clump overlap, palm/prop contact, grounded feet and scaffold intersections. Shadow-side contours may strengthen where they clarify mass; do not thicken every dark edge. Taper approaching open folds; lose selected lit shoulder/hair/world edges against matching values while keeping facial identity and action readable. Cloth folds follow suspension, tension and compression; hair remains unequal masses, not decorative curls.

Paint tooth belongs to pigment/substrate; illumination and cast/contact shadows remain separate decisions. Surface-attached strokes/texture and declared pose timing should prevent sliding/chatter; optional boil needs a held-pose comparison before adoption.

## Evaluation

**PAIRWISE:** A offers soft spatial daylight; C offers clearer drawing hierarchy. B buys focus but risks spectacle. Start by comparing A/C, not averaging them.

**CONTROLLED PROOF:** first same-pose value/color keys with only relationship changed; hold cast construction, framing and action constant. Then one provisional eight-second turn→reach→contact→visible correction→settle, with real cuff/hand changes, delayed hair/hem response, both mascots reacting and one scaffold element extending. Repeat with one replaceable element, unchanged choreography. Require actual consecutive-frame viewing and source/registration/export evidence; no moving textured still. Reverse the A starting preference if silhouettes/identity collapse; return to construction if grasp/cloth fails.
