# World reconfiguration asset contract

2026-09-30 · W2 · proposed study contract · input: [creative-spec v3-brief-04](../../media/showcase/v3/creative-spec.md), [phase plan](../showcase-creative-rework.md) D40.

**Proposed law:** one compatible kit reshapes into distinct task spaces while its material language, joins and actor scale remain recognizable. Soft asymmetric silhouettes may surround consistent joins. This specifies visible behavior; grid, topology and rendering method remain unchosen.

## Same kit, three configurations

Kit roles: terrain patch, path, opening/threshold, work surface and enclosure. Authored contours and unequal outer masses should feel inhabited. Reuse identifiable instances; expansion may add declared same-kit duplicates.

| Configuration            | Video-production task and visible arrangement                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Working nook             | The opening trio gathers inspiration and develops the shirt/hair study around one work surface, a partial enclosure and a short accessible route.                                            |
| Separated review spaces  | Rearrange those pieces into neighboring construction and color/script review pockets. A clear shared route and openings connect the pockets; boundaries distinguish what each review checks. |
| Shared assembly/ensemble | Open and reconnect the pockets into one gathering space for shot assembly and specialists. Extend terrain and routes with declared same-kit instances; preserve the earlier pieces visibly.  |

These are study candidates, not final beats or server-activity claims.

## Independent contracts

**Kit identity/interfaces:** each piece records type, instance identity, revision, authored silhouette/material roles, local orientation, join locations/directions, contact surface and actor-scale reference. Join compatibility is documented before arrangement. A replacement preserves its required interface or declares re-registration.

**Arrangement:** a separate record places instances, lists connected joins, routes, work/review purpose, usable openings and occupied/contact areas. It can change without silently changing the kit.

**Color/light:** source pigments and substrate belong to assets; named light origins, receiving surfaces, shadow behavior and A-daylight/B-focus staging belong to the scene. Reconfiguration cannot disguise a costume or mascot recolor as lighting.

**Camera/clock:** record view, staging and timing separately. Later animation evaluates pieces and actors on one declared scene time; camera movement does not prove rebuilding.

## Review evidence and failing control

The board tracks instances and compatible joins across three arrangements, with the trio at consistent scale. Explicit differences must show **remove** an enclosure, **replace** a work-surface variant, **reroute** a connection and **expand** a gathering area. Review checks distinguishable footprints, readable task boundaries, supported contact and unobstructed access.

Later proof must show actual moving reconfiguration, consecutive join/contact frames and a replacement repeat using declared unchanged inputs. Generated concepts demonstrate a depiction, not procedural rebuilding or runtime verification.

These are proposed reviewer criteria, not installed gates or autograders. Record viewed revision, observations, reviewer and correction. The fixed forest control fails adaptability when only decoration/view changes; its palette relationship remains accepted. Asymmetric join feasibility closes with the board and subsequent moving/contact proof.
