## Goals / proposed law and retirement

**Law (proposed): compatible soft pieces retain their identity while their arrangement makes a different task space readable.** Serves adaptability; risks kit repetition. Falsified if three arrangements differ only by decoration/camera, require unrelated replacement kits, break joins or lose actor scale.

Retire the active assumption that a lush fixed forest and dense microdetail are the production setting. Keep palette-light-01 as palette evidence and rejected world control. No code deletion or visual difference strip has been performed.

## Execution / directions and capability contract

1. **Patchwork workshop — recommended.** Animal Crossing placement + Tiny Glade accommodation; deprioritize Townscaper town density. Low rounded patches carry movable pavilion, table and display units. Moderate design effort; clear reuse and work surfaces. Risk: detached toy islands.
2. **Continuous elastic commons.** Tiny Glade responsiveness + soft artistic anchors; ignore explicit patch seams. One ground plane opens alcoves and stretches routes. Strong continuity; higher motion/contact burden. Risk: shapeless magical morphs hide what changed.
3. **Portable stage village.** Townscaper adjacency + Animal Crossing furniture; movable platforms gather around the trio. Clear episode staging with simple swaps; risk: theatrical scenery instead of inhabited world.

Patchwork wins over elastic ground because replacement is visible; it wins over portable stages because paths preserve everyday movement.
