## Prose Hygiene

Review the final draft after structure and facts are settled.

### Guidance criteria

- Use the project vocabulary and words a maintainer would say aloud.
- Keep at most one constructed brand line in the tagline position.
- Replace unnecessary em-dash connectors, mirrored slogans, triad flourishes, vague intensifiers, and uncommon verbs chosen for elegance.
- Prefer active verbs and concrete nouns over generic platform language.
- Preserve useful rhythm and personality; do not flatten precise prose into repetitive short sentences.
- Identify exact sentences to revise and explain the cadence issue briefly.

This gate is advisory. Factual and positioning gates take precedence.
