## Product Positioning Fidelity

Review public documentation against the implemented client and server boundary.

### Pass criteria

- The client remains the executor and source of tools, agents, memory, and native skills where applicable.
- Claude Prompts composes, sequences, versions, or validates workflows around client capabilities.
- Native skills remain useful and are not presented as replaced.
- The copy does not imply model training, automatic learning, general client memory, or autonomous repository execution.
- Capability claims map to documented behavior and client-specific behavior is scoped explicitly.
